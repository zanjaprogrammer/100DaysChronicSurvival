# Immune System Mechanics

## Auto Attack Behavior

### Targeting AI

**Priority System**:
1. **Threat Level**: Enemies that deal most damage
2. **Proximity**: Closest enemies
3. **Health**: Low health enemies (execute)
4. **Type Advantage**: Specialized targets (e.g., NK → Cancer)

**Unit-Specific Targeting**:
- Macrophages: Always closest
- T-Cells: Highest threat
- B-Cells: Farthest enemy
- NK Cells: Cancer cells first
- Neutrophils: Nearest swarm

### Movement AI

**Pathfinding**:
- A* algorithm for optimal routes
- Avoid hazards when possible
- Formation maintenance
- Collision avoidance

**Positioning**:
- Tanks move forward
- Ranged stay back
- Support finds safe spots
- Assassins flank

### Combat AI

**Attack Patterns**:
- Melee: Direct engagement
- Ranged: Kiting behavior
- AOE: Position for maximum hits
- Support: Optimal buff placement

**Survival Instincts**:
- Retreat when low health (if healer present)
- Dodge telegraphed attacks
- Use abilities optimally
- Call for backup (spawn signals)

## Immune Buff System

### Temporary Buffs

**Attack Buffs**:
- **Adrenaline Rush**: +50% attack speed (10s)
- **Immune Boost**: +30% damage (15s)
- **Coordinated Strike**: +20% damage when near allies (passive)

**Defense Buffs**:
- **Fortify**: +40% health (20s)
- **Regeneration**: Heal 5% per second (10s)
- **Shield**: Absorb next 100 damage (until broken)

**Utility Buffs**:
- **Haste**: +50% movement speed (8s)
- **Clarity**: Abilities cooldown 50% faster (15s)
- **Inspiration**: Nearby units gain +15% all stats (12s)

### Permanent Buffs (Card-Based)

**From Lifestyle Cards**:
- Good sleep: +10% immune regen
- Exercise: +10% attack speed
- Balanced diet: +15% immune health
- Stress reduction: +20% ability effectiveness
- Supplements: +10% all immune stats

### Buff Stacking

**Rules**:
- Same buff type: Refreshes duration, doesn't stack
- Different buff types: Stack additively
- Diminishing returns after 3 buffs
- Visual indicators for active buffs

## Immune Debuff System

### Disease-Inflicted Debuffs

**Diabetes Debuffs**:
- **Sugar Coating**: -30% attack speed
- **Insulin Resistance**: -20% damage
- **Metabolic Slowdown**: -40% movement speed
- **Energy Drain**: Lose 5 energy per second

**Hypertension Debuffs**:
- **Pressure Damage**: Take 10 damage per second
- **Vessel Rupture**: Random damage spikes
- **Instability**: 20% chance to miss attacks
- **Knockback**: Pushed by pressure waves

**Cancer Debuffs**:
- **Mutation Exposure**: -15% all stats
- **Toxic Environment**: Lose 3% health per second
- **Confusion**: Random targeting
- **Corruption**: Chance to turn against allies (rare)

### Environmental Debuffs

**Battlefield Hazards**:
- **Inflammation Zones**: -20% health, +10% damage taken
- **Toxic Puddles**: 5 damage per second
- **Pressure Zones**: Knockback every 3s
- **Glucose Puddles**: -50% movement speed

### Debuff Cleansing

**Methods**:
- Regulatory T-Cells: Cleanse nearby allies
- Emergency cards: Instant cleanse
- Time: Debuffs expire naturally
- Healing: Some heals remove debuffs

## Unit Placement Strategy

### Grid System

**5x5 Grid Layout**:
```
[Back-L] [Back-C] [Back-R]
[Mid-L]  [Mid-C]  [Mid-R]
[Front-L][Front-C][Front-R]
```

**Zone Characteristics**:
- **Front**: High danger, protects back
- **Mid**: Balanced, flexible
- **Back**: Safe, for ranged/support
- **Left/Right**: Flanking opportunities

### Formation Strategies

**Tank Wall**:
```
[  -  ] [  -  ] [  -  ]
[  -  ] [  -  ] [  -  ]
[Tank] [Tank] [Tank]
```
- Macrophages in front
- Ranged behind
- Support in back

**Assassin Flanks**:
```
[Support] [  -  ] [Support]
[Ranged]  [Tank] [Ranged]
[T-Cell]  [  -  ] [T-Cell]
```
- T-Cells on flanks
- Tank in center
- Ranged and support protected

**Balanced Formation**:
```
[B-Cell] [Dendri] [B-Cell]
[Neutro] [Macro]  [Neutro]
[T-Cell] [NK Cell][T-Cell]
```
- Mix of all types
- Adaptable to threats
- Good for unknown enemies

## Regeneration System

### Passive Regeneration

**Base Regen**:
- All immune cells: 1% health per second (out of combat)
- In combat: 0.5% health per second
- Modified by immune strength stat

**Regen Modifiers**:
- High immune strength: +100% regen
- Good sleep: +50% regen
- Low energy: -50% regen
- High inflammation: -30% regen

### Active Regeneration

**Healing Sources**:
- Regulatory T-Cells: 10 HP/s in radius
- Healing cards: Instant 50% health
- Safe zones: 5 HP/s
- Between rounds: Full heal (if sleep quality >60)

### Spawn Regeneration

**New Unit Spawning**:
- Spawn rate: 1 unit per 10 seconds (base)
- Modified by immune strength
- Energy cost per spawn
- Max units on field: 10 (base)

**Spawn Modifiers**:
- High immune: +50% spawn rate
- Low energy: -50% spawn rate
- Dendritic cell present: +30% spawn rate
