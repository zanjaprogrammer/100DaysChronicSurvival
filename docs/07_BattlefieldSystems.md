# Battlefield Systems

## Organ-Based Arenas

### 1. Bloodstream Arena (Default)
**Theme**: Circulatory Highway

**Layout**:
- Open central area
- Blood vessel lanes (3-4 lanes)
- Flow direction indicators
- Valve choke points

**Mechanics**:
- **Blood Flow**: Units move faster with flow, slower against
- **Current Zones**: Passive movement in direction
- **Vessel Walls**: Boundaries that can rupture
- **Oxygen Zones**: Buff areas for immune cells

**Hazards**:
- Vessel ruptures (hypertension)
- Glucose puddles (diabetes)
- Tumor growths (cancer)
- Clot formations

**Visual Design**:
- Red/pink color palette
- Flowing liquid effects
- Pulsing vessel walls
- Cellular traffic

**Best For**: General combat, all disease types

---

### 2. Liver Arena
**Theme**: Detoxification Plant

**Layout**:
- Central processing area
- Detox zones (safe areas)
- Toxic waste zones (hazards)
- Bile ducts (pathways)

**Mechanics**:
- **Detox Zones**: Heal immune cells, remove debuffs
- **Toxic Puddles**: Damage over time
- **Filter System**: Periodic cleansing wave
- **Bile Flow**: Pushes units in direction

**Hazards**:
- Toxic accumulation (high toxicity)
- Liver damage zones
- Bile overflow
- Fatty deposits (diabetes)

**Visual Design**:
- Brown/green color palette
- Industrial/organic hybrid
- Filtering effects
- Toxic glow

**Best For**: Cancer battles, toxicity management

---

### 3. Pancreas Arena
**Theme**: Insulin Factory

**Layout**:
- Beta cell clusters (objectives)
- Insulin production zones
- Glucose regulation areas
- Islet of Langerhans (central structure)

**Mechanics**:
- **Insulin Zones**: Reduce blood sugar, buff immune
- **Beta Cell Protection**: Bonus objective
- **Glucose Overflow**: Hazard if blood sugar high
- **Production Boost**: Temporary buff zones

**Hazards**:
- Glucose flooding
- Beta cell destruction
- Insulin resistance zones
- Metabolic chaos

**Visual Design**:
- Yellow/amber color palette
- Factory-like structures
- Insulin droplets
- Glucose crystals

**Best For**: Diabetes battles, blood sugar management

---

### 4. Heart Arena
**Theme**: Cardiac Chamber

**Layout**:
- Four chambers (atria and ventricles)
- Valve gates (choke points)
- Coronary arteries (lanes)
- Pacemaker node (central point)

**Mechanics**:
- **Heartbeat Rhythm**: Periodic pulses affect gameplay
- **Valve Gates**: Open/close periodically
- **Pressure Chambers**: High/low pressure zones
- **Cardiac Cycle**: Systole (attack phase) / Diastole (rest phase)

**Hazards**:
- Pressure waves
- Arrhythmia events
- Valve malfunction
- Coronary blockage

**Visual Design**:
- Deep red color palette
- Pulsing chambers
- Rhythmic animations
- Electrical signals

**Best For**: Hypertension battles, pressure management

---

### 5. Lung Arena
**Theme**: Oxygen Exchange

**Layout**:
- Alveoli clusters (small chambers)
- Bronchial pathways
- Oxygen-rich zones (blue)
- Carbon dioxide zones (gray)

**Mechanics**:
- **Breathing Cycle**: Inhale (expand) / Exhale (contract)
- **Oxygen Zones**: Buff immune cells (+20% stats)
- **CO2 Zones**: Debuff all units
- **Air Flow**: Directional movement

**Hazards**:
- Tumor growths (cancer)
- Inflammation (reduced oxygen)
- Mucus blockage
- Collapsed alveoli

**Visual Design**:
- Pink/blue color palette
- Spongy texture
- Breathing animation
- Gas exchange effects

**Best For**: Cancer battles, oxygen management

## Blood Vessel Lanes

### Lane System

**Lane Types**:
1. **Arterial Lanes** (Red): Fast flow, high pressure
2. **Venous Lanes** (Blue): Slow flow, low pressure
3. **Capillary Lanes** (Pink): Narrow, exchange zones

**Lane Mechanics**:
- **Flow Direction**: Units move faster with flow
- **Lane Switching**: Strategic positioning
- **Choke Points**: Narrow sections
- **Branching**: Multiple path options

**Strategic Depth**:
- Flanking opportunities
- Ambush points
- Escape routes
- Positioning advantages

## Dynamic Battlefield Events

### Environmental Events

**Heartbeat Pulse**:
- Periodic arena-wide effect
- Pushes all units slightly
- Rhythm-based gameplay
- Visual/audio cue

**Breathing Cycle** (Lung Arena):
- Inhale: Arena expands, more space
- Exhale: Arena contracts, units closer
- Affects positioning strategy
- 5-second cycle

**Digestive Wave** (Intestine Arena):
- Peristalsis pushes units forward
- Can't resist the wave
- Strategic timing required
- 10-second cycle

**Neural Storm** (Brain Arena):
- Random buff/debuff waves
- Unpredictable effects
- High stress = more frequent
- Chaotic gameplay

**Filtration Cycle** (Kidney Arena):
- Periodic toxin removal
- Cleanses debuffs
- Heals immune cells
- 15-second cycle

---

### Hazard Zones

**Glucose Puddles** (Diabetes):
- Size: 2x2 tiles
- Effect: -50% movement speed
- Duration: 30 seconds
- Spawned by: High blood sugar, enemy deaths

**Pressure Waves** (Hypertension):
- Size: Arena-wide
- Effect: Knockback + 20 damage
- Frequency: Every 10 seconds (high BP)
- Spawned by: High blood pressure, enemy abilities

**Toxic Clouds** (Cancer):
- Size: 3x3 tiles
- Effect: 5 damage per second, mutation chance
- Duration: 20 seconds
- Spawned by: High toxicity, enemy deaths

**Inflammation Zones**:
- Size: 2x2 tiles
- Effect: 10 damage per second to all units
- Duration: 15 seconds
- Spawned by: High inflammation, combat

**Vessel Ruptures** (Hypertension):
- Size: 2x2 tiles
- Effect: 15 damage per second
- Duration: Until healed
- Spawned by: High blood pressure, pressure waves

**Tumor Growths** (Cancer):
- Size: 3x3 tiles
- Effect: Spawns enemies, blocks movement
- Duration: Until destroyed
- Spawned by: High cancer progression

---

### Corrupted Areas

**Corruption Mechanics**:
- Spreads from disease nodes
- Buffs enemies in area (+15% stats)
- Debuffs immune cells (-10% stats)
- Visual transformation

**Corruption Stages**:
1. **Light Corruption** (0-25%):
   - Slight discoloration
   - Minor stat changes
   - Reversible easily

2. **Medium Corruption** (26-50%):
   - Visible damage
   - Moderate stat changes
   - Requires effort to cleanse

3. **Heavy Corruption** (51-75%):
   - Severe damage
   - Major stat changes
   - Hazards spawn
   - Difficult to cleanse

4. **Critical Corruption** (76-100%):
   - Arena transformation
   - Extreme stat changes
   - Constant hazards
   - Nearly impossible to cleanse

**Cleansing Methods**:
- Destroy disease nodes
- Use cleansing cards
- Improve body components
- Time-based decay (if conditions improve)

---

**Hydration Zones**:

## Arena Expansion

### Expansion Mechanics

**Progressive Expansion**:
- Arena starts small (5x5 grid)
- Expands as disease progresses
- Day 1-25: 5x5 grid
- Day 26-50: 7x7 grid
- Day 51-75: 9x9 grid
- Day 76-100: 11x11 grid

**Expansion Triggers**:
- Disease stage increases
- Multiple diseases active
- Boss battles
- Special events

**Strategic Impact**:
- More space = more enemies
- Harder to defend all areas
- More positioning options
- Increased complexity

---

### Multi-Arena Battles (Late Game)

**Metastasis Mechanic**:
- Cancer spreads to multiple organs
- Fight in 2-3 arenas simultaneously
- Split immune forces
- Strategic prioritization

**Arena Switching**:
- Quick switch between arenas
- Pause one, active in another
- Time pressure
- Multitasking challenge

**Synergy Effects**:
- Actions in one arena affect others
- Connected systems
- Holistic strategy
- Realistic biology
