# Core Concept

## Genre
- **Roguelike Survival**: Permadeath, procedural generation, meta progression
- **Auto Battler**: Unit placement dan strategi, combat otomatis
- **Strategy**: Resource management, tactical decision making
- **Card Management**: Deck building, card synergies
- **Educational**: Biological accuracy, health literacy

## High Concept
**"Survive 100 Days: Your Body is the Battlefield"**

Pemain mengelola tubuh manusia sebagai ekosistem biologis yang terus diserang oleh tiga penyakit kronis utama:
- **Diabetes Melitus**: Serangan metabolik yang overwhelming
- **Hipertensi**: Tekanan yang merusak sistem kardiovaskular
- **Kanker**: Mutasi sel yang tidak terkendali

### Core Pillars
1. **Biological Warfare**: Tubuh sebagai arena pertempuran dinamis
2. **Lifestyle Consequences**: Setiap keputusan memiliki dampak biologis nyata
3. **Unpredictable Reality**: Random events mencerminkan kehidupan nyata
4. **Educational Immersion**: Belajar melalui gameplay, bukan teks
5. **Strategic Depth**: Multiple systems yang saling terhubung

## Core Gameplay Loop

### Phase 1: Battle Phase (Auto Battler)
**Duration**: 30-60 seconds per round

**Mechanics**:
- Pathogen muncul dari infection nodes
- Immune cells menyerang otomatis berdasarkan AI behavior
- Player mengatur positioning dan build sebelum battle
- Real-time tactical adjustments (limited)

**Player Actions**:
- Pre-battle: Unit placement, loadout selection
- During battle: Emergency cards, repositioning (cooldown-based)
- Post-battle: Collect resources, assess damage

**Inspiration**: 
- TABS (Totally Accurate Battle Simulator) - physics-based chaos
- Vampire Survivors - overwhelming enemy waves
- Auto Chess - strategic positioning

### Phase 2: Lifestyle Decision Phase (Card Selection)
**Duration**: 15-30 seconds

**Mechanics**:
- Player diberi **3 Action Cards** (positive lifestyle choices)
- Each card shows:
  - Body component effects (↑↓ indicators)
  - Immune system buffs
  - Disease resistance changes
  - Synergy potential with previous choices

**Card Categories**:
- **Nutrition**: Food choices, hydration
- **Exercise**: Physical activity levels
- **Rest**: Sleep quality, recovery
- **Mental Health**: Stress management, meditation
- **Medical**: Supplements, check-ups
- **Social**: Support systems, relationships

**Strategic Depth**:
- Build synergies (e.g., Exercise + Sleep = enhanced recovery)
- Counter specific diseases
- Balance short-term vs long-term benefits
- Risk assessment

### Phase 3: Random Body Behavior Event (Chaos Factor)
**Duration**: 5-10 seconds (animation + effect)

**Mechanics**:
- Tubuh melakukan keputusan random yang **tidak bisa dikontrol**
- Weighted probability based on:
  - Current body components status
  - Disease progression levels
  - Previous lifestyle choices
  - Genetic predisposition

**Event Types**:

**Negative Events** (60% probability):
- 🍔 Junk Food Craving: ↑ Blood Sugar, ↓ Insulin Efficiency
- 🌙 Insomnia/Begadang: ↓ Sleep Quality, ↑ Stress
- 💼 Work Stress: ↑ Stress, ↑ Blood Pressure
- 🚬 Smoking Relapse: ↑ Toxicity, ↑ Inflammation
- 🍺 Alcohol Consumption: ↓ Immune Strength, ↑ Toxicity
- 📱 Screen Time Overload: ↓ Sleep Quality, ↑ Stress
- 🍕 Late Night Snacking: ↑ Blood Sugar, ↓ Metabolism

**Positive Events** (30% probability):
- 🚶 Spontaneous Walk: ↑ Energy, ↓ Stress
- 😴 Good Sleep: ↑ Sleep Quality, ↑ Immune Regen
- 💧 Hydration Habit: ↑ Hydration, ↓ Inflammation
- 🧘 Mindful Moment: ↓ Stress, ↑ Hormone Balance
- 🥗 Healthy Meal: ↑ Energy, ↓ Blood Sugar

**Neutral Events** (10% probability):
- Weather changes
- Social interactions
- Minor daily activities

**Why This Matters**:
- Creates **tension** and unpredictability
- Reflects **real-life struggles** with maintaining healthy habits
- Prevents "perfect play" scenarios
- Teaches that health management is ongoing, not absolute
- Adds **replayability** through variance

## Win & Lose Condition

### Win Condition
**Survive for 100 Days**

**Victory Tiers**:
- **Bronze**: Survived 100 days (any condition)
- **Silver**: Survived 100 days + all diseases below 50%
- **Gold**: Survived 100 days + all diseases below 25%
- **Platinum**: Survived 100 days + all diseases below 10%
- **Perfect**: Survived 100 days + no disease activated

**Rewards**:
- Unlock new immune cells
- Unlock new action cards
- Unlock genetic modifiers
- Unlock challenge modes
- Meta progression points

### Lose Condition
**Disease Activation**

Game over jika salah satu disease meter mencapai **Critical Threshold**:

**Diabetes Activated** (≥80% meter):
- Insulin system collapse
- Metabolic shutdown
- Overwhelming glucose enemies

**Hypertension Critical** (≥80% meter):
- Cardiovascular failure
- Vessel rupture events
- Pressure boss spawns

**Cancer Developed** (≥80% meter):
- Tumor formation
- Uncontrollable mutation
- Immune system overwhelmed

**Multiple Disease Synergy**:
- If 2 diseases reach 60%: Synergy effects activate
- If 3 diseases reach 50%: "System Collapse" mode begins

### Soft Fail States
**Warning Zones** (60-79% meter):
- Visual warnings intensify
- Disease-specific debuffs activate
- Boss pre-spawn warnings
- Last chance recovery cards appear

## Replayability

### Run Variance
1. **Genetic Starting Conditions**: Random inherited traits
2. **Random Event Pool**: Different event sequences each run
3. **Card Draw RNG**: Different lifestyle options available
4. **Disease Spawn Patterns**: Procedural enemy waves
5. **Node Placement**: Dynamic battlefield layout

### Meta Progression
1. **Permanent Unlocks**: New units, cards, abilities
2. **Knowledge Tree**: Learn disease mechanics for bonuses
3. **Achievement System**: Specific goals for unlocks

### Build Diversity
1. **Tank/Sustain Build**: Focus on defense and recovery
2. **Aggressive Immune Build**: High damage, high risk
3. **Metabolism Control Build**: Counter diabetes specifically
4. **Cardiovascular Build**: Counter hypertension
5. **Anti-Cancer Build**: Focus on toxicity control
6. **Balanced Build**: Jack of all trades


## Educational Integration

### Learning Through Play
- **Cause & Effect**: See immediate biological consequences
- **System Interconnection**: Understand how body systems relate
- **Lifestyle Impact**: Experience long-term health decisions
- **Disease Progression**: Learn how chronic diseases develop
- **Prevention Strategies**: Discover effective health habits

### Subtle Education
- No forced tutorials or text walls
- Information embedded in gameplay
- Visual feedback for biological processes
- Natural discovery through experimentation
- Reward understanding with better performance

### Real-World Application
- Accurate biological representations
- Evidence-based health information
- Practical lifestyle recommendations
- Risk factor awareness
- Empathy for chronic disease management
